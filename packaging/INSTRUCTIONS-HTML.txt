DISTRIMATCHER – test package (single-file edition)
==================================================

What it is
  Distrimatcher reads distribution agreements (PDF / ZIP, also scanned documents), compares them with fund
  data and lists every share class that is NOT covered by an agreement – with a focus on share classes that
  pay retrocessions. It is ONE HTML file: no installation, no server, no admin rights. Everything runs in
  your browser on your own PC; agreement contents never leave the computer.

Contents of this package
  Distrimatcher.html             the application – double-click to open in Edge or Chrome
  Distrimatcher-Brochure.pdf     product brochure and illustrated user manual (14 pages)
  INSTRUCTIONS.txt               this file
  Test data\
    Test-Agreements.zip          all 17 test agreements in one ZIP (upload this in step 1)
    Agreements (PDF)\            the same 17 agreements as single PDF files
      sec_public\                11 real, public agreements / schedules from SEC EDGAR (US funds)
      synthetic\                 6 fictitious EU / Swiss agreements (incl. 2 scanned documents for OCR)
    Fund_list_with_retro.csv     fund list (ISINs + retrocession) for the fictitious fund houses
    SEC_dataset_2026.csv         public SEC "Investment Company Series and Class" dataset (US funds)
  Graphics\                      logo, business concept and architecture graphics (PNG)

Try it (about 5 minutes)
  1  Double-click Distrimatcher.html. The app opens empty.
  2  Agreements: drag "Test data\Test-Agreements.zip" onto the upload area.
     17 agreements are read; the two *_SCAN.pdf files are read with OCR (takes a little longer).
  3  Overview:
       SEC box  -> "Import SEC dataset" -> Test data\SEC_dataset_2026.csv
                -> "Suggest from agreements" -> "Load selected"
       File import -> "Import file" -> Test data\Fund_list_with_retro.csv
     The waterfall shows Fund IDs -> ISINs -> found in agreements -> covered -> gaps.
     The (i) button opens the manual.
  4  Watchlist: confirm / dismiss gaps, "Add to agreement", "Open agreement".
  5  Provider e-mail: choose e.g. "Seealp Swiss Funds" -> "Download .eml" -> opens as draft in Outlook.
  6  Activity log: everything that happened. "Save project" (top right) keeps all data in one file.

What the fictitious test agreements contain
  Alpenblick Invest SICAV (LU)      Global distribution agreement (EN) + German amendment
  Nordlicht Funds ICAV (IE)         German distribution agreement, scanned (OCR)
  Seealp Swiss Funds (CH)           Swiss "Vertriebsvertrag" with Bestandespflegekommission (DE)
                                    + scanned side letter adding a hedged share class (OCR)
  Kestrel Point Funds ICAV (IE)     Amendment No. 2 with a new schedule and trail commission
  All companies, funds, ISINs and people in these documents are FICTITIOUS (check digits are valid so that
  the validation works). The SEC documents are public filings of US fund companies.

Notes
  - Your data stays in this browser on this PC. Clearing the browser's site data deletes it – use
    "Save project" to keep a copy.
  - Only the GLEIF button needs internet access. Everything else works offline.
  - Problems? The app shows notes per document; see also the brochure, page 12 (FAQ).
